---
title: "Overlays"
weight: 5
---

Components that float above the page, mounted and unmounted with a visible enter and exit
via the core [`usePresence`](/guide/hooks/#usepresence) hook: `Modal` and `Toast` (layered
through a core [`portal`](/guide/components/) into `document.body`), and `Tooltip` (a hint
anchored inline to its trigger).
